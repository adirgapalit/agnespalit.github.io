Hi, I'm Agnes Palit, a London-based Software Engineer with experience building full-stack web applications, cloud-based systems, data-driven tools, and interactive user experiences.

My experience spans frontend and backend development, CMS integrations, cloud infrastructure, database management, performance optimisation, usability testing, and production deployment. I have worked with technologies including PHP, Laravel, JavaScript, Vue.js, jQuery, MySQL, AWS, Python, Java, and MATLAB.

I hold an MSc in Advanced Software Engineering from The University of Sheffield, where I graduated with Distinction, and a Bachelor of Computer Science in Information Technology from Telkom University, where I graduated Cum Laude.

My academic and research background includes machine learning, image recognition, traffic sign detection, wearable sensor data analytics, and data visualisation.

I enjoy building reliable, user-focused, and maintainable software. This portfolio highlights selected projects, professional experience, research work, and technical skills across software engineering, web development, cloud computing, and machine learning.
